# Winds Ahead · 연운경 (燕雲鏡)

[English](README.md) · [한국어](README.ko.md)

A bilingual fan reference for **Where Winds Meet** cosmetics: discover appearances released on the China server, view photos and videos, and follow confirmed global releases and evidence-based estimates.

**Status:** Planning v0.1. No application, live catalog, or deployment exists yet. The documents describe proposed behavior, not shipped features.

Repository: [developdh/Winds-Ahead](https://github.com/developdh/Winds-Ahead) (private).

## Product principles

- Put the cosmetic gallery and release information first.
- Keep China-server facts, global-server facts, and editorial forecasts separate.
- Show evidence, date precision, and revision history. Unknown dates stay unknown.
- Support English and Korean throughout the site and project documentation; preserve original Chinese names for identification and search.
