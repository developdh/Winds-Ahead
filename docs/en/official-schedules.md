# Official schedules and discontinued estimates

[한국어](../ko/official-schedules.md)

On October 2, 2026 (America/New_York), the user discontinued editorial release forecasting to reduce confusion. This decision supersedes earlier forecast-related requirements and proposals throughout the project.

The archive, roadmap, calendar, site description and magazine use official schedules and verified regional release records. Unannounced Global dates remain unknown. Announcements do not automatically become verified releases when their dates pass. China-server facts never establish a Global release date.

Do not generate, revise, reactivate, publish or review future release estimates during maintenance. Continue checking official names, acquisition, prices, deadlines and missing usable galleries through the existing workflow. Do not create a replacement schedule or enable automatic merging.

The eight latest forecast series received explicit withdrawal revisions; all preceding records remain in `site/content/forecasts.json` for audit only and are not imported into the visitor application. Historical research and release notes remain records of past work, not current product requirements.

Magazine issue 01 edition 5 removes the forecast article and cover mention. Its six share pages contain the cover, four officially scheduled appearances and the site introduction. Earlier exports are removed from current public assets and remain recoverable in Git history; older download URLs therefore stop being served. Already downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Validation and publishing follow [releasing](releasing.md). This is a policy and interface removal, not a new source verification or a claim that previous estimates were accurate.
