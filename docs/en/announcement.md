Introducing Winds Ahead / 연운경 — a Where Winds Meet cosmetic archive and release roadmap

Hello! I built Winds Ahead, an independent fan site that brings Where Winds Meet cosmetic information together.

The project started as a way to preview China-server cosmetics and compare their names and announcements with Global releases. It supports English and Korean on desktop and mobile.

Website: https://windsahead.com

What can you explore?

The archive currently contains 685 appearance records and 554 reference photos, covering outfits, hair, weapons, effects, accessories and mounts. Photos and details are still missing for some records; source-backed information is being added as it is reviewed.

Select a cosmetic to open its details in a popup without leaving the catalog. Browse photos and compare appearances while keeping your place in the list. Some outfit galleries now have 6–10 images showing different views and details. Effect videos load only when you press play.

Prices and acquisition information

Where sources provide them, records show the required currency and amount, shops or events, acquisition conditions and outfit components. China and Global prices and conditions are kept separate. Unknown prices and components are not filled in with guesses.

Search and filter by appearance category or release server, then sort by China or Global release date. Save favorites to a watchlist or share an individual cosmetic by link. Watchlists are stored in the browser you use.

Upcoming appearances and release forecasts

Officially announced releases and editorial forecasts have distinct labels. Explore countdowns in the catalog, a release roadmap and a monthly calendar. Forecasts are not official dates and may change after later announcements. Older announcements whose actual release has not been verified are also labeled separately.

The values behind Winds Ahead

Trustworthy information comes first. China and Global records, official facts and editorial forecasts stay distinct. Unknowns are labeled honestly, and original sources remain available for readers to check.

Rich information should be simple to use. The goal is to help people compare photos and essential details without repeatedly navigating between pages or working through unnecessary controls.

Browsing should stay light and responsive. As the archive grows, image sizes, loading behavior, mobile layouts and interaction speed remain ongoing priorities.

The archive should be an open, shared record. Information is published so anyone can inspect and correct it. A source link or a small correction is a valuable contribution.

An open-source project anyone can contribute to

The code and documentation are public on GitHub. Contributions are welcome, including code, translations, corrections to names, prices or dates, and original sources for missing cosmetics and photos. Game images, videos and other third-party materials retain their own rights and are separate from the code's open-source license.

The project does not generate revenue. It is a non-monetized fan project with no advertising or paid features, and it is not an official game service.

GitHub: https://github.com/developdh/Winds-Ahead

There is still more to improve. Please share missing appearances, incorrect information or anything that feels inconvenient to use. Thank you for taking a look!
