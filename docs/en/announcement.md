**Winds Ahead: a Where Winds Meet cosmetic archive (China + Global / open source)**

I built **Winds Ahead / 연운경** to bring China-server cosmetics and Global release information together.

👉 https://windsahead.com

- Browse photos and videos of outfits, hair, weapons and effects.
- Check prices, currencies, acquisition methods and regional release status.
- Compare appearances in a dialog, save favorites and share links.
- Explore a calendar and roadmap that distinguish official dates from estimates.

English and Korean are supported, including mobile layouts. Forecasts are unofficial; missing photos and unverified details are still being filled in.

**This is an open-source project, and anyone can contribute.** Code, translations, corrections and source references are all welcome.

**The project does not generate revenue.** It is a non-monetized fan project with no ads or paid features.

GitHub: https://github.com/developdh/Winds-Ahead

Please share corrections or anything that feels awkward to use.
