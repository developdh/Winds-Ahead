# 0.3.4 — Official schedules only

[한국어](../../ko/releases/0.3.4.md) · [Website](https://windsahead.com/en/calendar) · [Policy](../official-schedules.md) · [PR #45](https://github.com/developdh/Winds-Ahead/pull/45)

Discontinued editorial release forecasting at the user's request. Removed estimates, their filter, rationale and public revision history from the roadmap/calendar; removed estimated countdowns from the archive and estimate copy from the about page and metadata. Old `kind=forecast` links now show official records. Unannounced dates remain unknown; official schedules, regional facts, acquisition, prices, galleries and deadlines remain available.

Withdrew all eight forecast series with new audit revisions, preserving every earlier record. Current maintenance instructions prohibit generating or reactivating forecasts. Earlier planning and research documents explicitly defer to the new policy. No new recurring task or automatic merge was enabled; the scheduler settings could not be read through the available automation interface, so no scheduler change is claimed.

Magazine issue 01 edition 5 removes the estimate article, cover mention and forecast image. Its six bilingual share pages contain the cover, four official appearance pages and the site introduction. Previous exports are removed from public assets and retained in Git history; their old download URLs are retired. Correction history remains visible, and existing downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Validation: type checking, 44 domain/media tests, content/export validation, preservation of prior forecast records and the root production build passed. Chrome checks covered both languages at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px across the legacy forecast URL, calendar, previously forecast item detail, about and magazine views. Checked popup open/Escape close, month navigation, six decoded previews and ZIP downloads per language. No horizontal overflow, broken loaded images or page errors were observed. Cover and final share pages were visually inspected in both languages. Existing framework/large-chunk warnings remain; physical devices, Firefox/WebKit, field performance and community compression were not tested.
