# Winds Ahead v0.1.0

[한국어](../../ko/releases/0.1.0.md) · [Website](https://windsahead.com) · [GitHub release](https://github.com/developdh/Winds-Ahead/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

The first public release of **Winds Ahead / 연운경 (燕雲鏡)**, an independent Where Winds Meet cosmetic archive and release roadmap.

## Included

- English/Korean browsing, a remembered language choice and a responsive dark interface.
- 685 appearance records, 554 photos across 370 appearances and 395 linked wiki references. Search names and aliases, filter categories and servers, and sort by regional release date.
- In-place detail popups with large photos, gallery navigation, acquisition locations, currencies, prices and outfit components. Share individual appearances and save a browser-local watchlist.
- Separate China/Global release states, official upcoming dates and unofficial AI-assisted forecasts, with both roadmap and monthly calendar views.
- Photos loaded as needed and effect videos loaded on request, without bulk autoplay.
- Public browsing without sign-in, contribution guides, bilingual documentation and open-source application code.

## Recent corrections included

Expanded outfit galleries and official Global social references, including the Bane of Life hair/accessory set. Expired announcements awaiting release verification no longer appear in the upcoming roadmap; source records and calendar history are preserved. See [the media/social update](https://github.com/developdh/Winds-Ahead/pull/20) and [the roadmap fix](https://github.com/developdh/Winds-Ahead/pull/23).

## Validation and limits

Type checking, all 32 domain/media tests, content validation and the production build passed for the application changes. The upcoming roadmap was checked in English/Korean, and anonymous production access returned HTTP 200. Detailed scope is recorded in the [launch review](../public-launch.md) and [roadmap review](../release-roadmap.md).

The archive is still growing: 315 records lack a reviewed image, and some names, prices and release dates remain unknown. Forecasts are unofficial, may change, and do not establish release confirmation. Physical-device coverage and field performance targets are not yet verified.
