# Winds Ahead v0.3.1

[한국어](../../ko/releases/0.3.1.md) · [Issue 01](https://windsahead.com/en/magazine/issue-01)

Re-edited the community issue as eight photographic fashion pages per language. A full-bleed female cover, large serif headlines, ivory/navy/sage palettes, detail crops and Lian Tai He's female front/back diptych replace the active typography-only downloads. Each officially scheduled October 4 appearance has its own photo story, so readers can see every featured appearance without following links. The information date and forecast revision 3 are unchanged; edition 2 records the visual revision.

The website offers 1080px JPGs and ZIPs, loading small, lazy WebP previews with reserved dimensions. Importing export metadata from content fixes Vite's development error caused by importing from public. First-edition PNGs, ZIPs and metadata remain at their historical paths. Font subsets include export copy and retain OFL notices.

The user requested existing official CN photos in the community edition. Original paths, URLs and unknown permission status are recorded; complete originals remain accessible in the archive. These are not verified live Global photos, and attribution does not establish redistribution permission. Official schedules, estimates and unknown deadline timezones stay separate. No forecasts, schedules or community posts were added.

Type checks, 46 existing tests, content/export validation, forecast-history validation and the root production build passed on Windows. Chrome KO320/390px and EN360/1440px checks passed without overflow, broken loaded images or page errors. Checked eight decoded previews, ZIP responses, navigation, language switching, archive links, source disclosure, paired metadata/sitemap and unknown-issue 404. All 16 JPG/WebP pairs passed dimensions and budgets; JPGs are about 350–480KB and previews 29–42KB. Both languages were visually inspected. Existing framework and large-chunk warnings remain; physical devices, platform upload/compression and field performance were not tested.
